# Measuring the tempeatur and humidity in the air
Two importent variables in the weather forecast are the relative temperatur and the humidity of the air. 

## DHT11 temperatur and humdity sensor
The DHT11 is a dual purpose digital sensor. It is capable of measuring the relative humidity and temperatur of the surrounding air. 





It is commenly used in HVAC systems, automotitve industrie, IoT applications and small weather stations like this station here.
